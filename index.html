Stop Building AI

Make a deal to pause AI. On November 18, President Trump will travel to China to meet Xi Jinping at the APEC summit. It's time to start negotiating an AI treaty.

See the route Why we march

The demand

An international treaty that pauses AI until democratic decisions are made about how, and whether, to build it, and until there is scientific consensus that it's safe to proceed.

Why we march

A big tent for AI action

On July 11, 13 organizations marched on AI companies in San Francisco, demanding they stop developing more powerful AI without our consent. Now AI CEOs are saying they would like governments to intervene. So we're organizing another march on November 14, asking world leaders to step in.

The race is the problem

Job loss, AI surveillance, environmental damage, loss of control. All these risks come from the AI arms race between AI labs, but also between the US and China, racing each other to build ever more powerful models without enough time to make them safe or for society to adapt.
